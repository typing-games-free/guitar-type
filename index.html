Guitar Type

Home row variant of the popular Guitar Typing Music Game. Hit keys to become a rock hero typing artist! Pick your grade level, then a song.

Keys: A S D F J K L ; — press the matching key when a note reaches the line. Hit notes to play the melody. Esc returns to this menu.
